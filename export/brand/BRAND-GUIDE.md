# Adashenmu brand guide

**Name:** Adashenmu (Hausa: "our adashe"). Always written in lowercase in the logo: **adashenmu**.
**Tagline:** Save together. Collect on time.

## Logo

The mark is an illustrated **asusu** (clay savings pot) in Adashe Green, with:
- a forest rim and a darker neck
- a cream zigzag band (from Hausa wall and calabash patterns)
- a gold band and cream dots
- a soft highlight
- a gold coin dropping in

**Lockup rules:**
- The pot sits beside the wordmark, never above it.
- The gap between them is about 1/10 of the pot height.
- Wordmark: Manrope ExtraBold, lowercase, slight negative tracking (about −3.5%).
- Clear space around the logo equals the coin's height.
- Minimum lockup width: 120 px.

**Don't:** recolour the logo outside these versions, stretch it, rotate it, move the coin, or change the spacing between the pot and the name.

## Type

**Manrope** for headings, the wordmark and naira amounts. **Inter** for body and UI text.
